## Decisions taken here

- **The searcher is a `PlayerFilter`, not a `PlayerRef`.** A trigger matches the player an event
  happened to; it does not name one in advance. `Draws(PlayerRef Who)` reads `EachOpponent` as a
  matcher by convention, but the newer triggers describe the player (`DealsDamage.ToPlayer`), and the
  brief's shape is the one that cannot be misread as "cause an opponent to search".
- **`Searches` carries no zone and no "whose library".** Every printed sentence of this shape is about
  the searcher's own library. A zone field would be a second thing to get wrong on every card, and a
  card that watches a search of somebody else's library does not exist in the corpus yet.
- **The secret choice is one record, and the reveal is inside it.** CR 101.4's simultaneity is the
  whole of what the card says: a `ForEachPlayer` around a `ChooseValue(Number)` asks in APNAP order,
  so every player after the first would choose knowing the answers before theirs, which plays
  differently and is a different card. A separate "reveal the chosen numbers" step would have nothing
  to reveal, because the numbers are already stored.
- **`HighestChosen`/`LowestChosen` are amounts and `PlayerWhoChose` is a player reference, not one
  record with a role flag.** The card reads the number in one clause and the players in another, and
  the two clauses are separately optional — Wheel of Misfortune reads the highest number without
  naming who chose it, then names who chose the lowest without reading the number. `PlayerWhoChose`
  names every player who chose that extreme, so a tie needs no extra field.
- **L035 is the pairing check, both ways.** An extreme read from a key no `ChooseNumberSecretly` on
  the ability asked for reads nothing, and a secret choice nothing reads back is a dropped sentence.
  The three readers are also added to `Linter.IsConsumer`, so their keys are not mistaken for keys
  they produce (which would have let a bogus `ChosenNumber` key pass L003).
- **The two turn counts are amounts and not predicates.** The predicates `DrawnThisTurn` and
  `EnteredThisTurn` describe an object that is still somewhere to be described; these count events the
  turn recorded, which is why a discarded card and a sacrificed land still count. Both were needed by
  one card, so they are added together and worded as each other's mirror.
- **`BecomesCopyOfNamed` is its own record and not a `Name` on `BecomesCopyOf`.** `BecomesCopyOf.Of`
  is a `Selector` and required; making it optional beside a name would admit a copy effect that names
  neither and would put two unrelated readers behind one record. A separate record also makes the one
  place a card name is data visible in the name of the record.
- **The name is checked against the corpus by the pipeline, not by the linter.** `MtgEngine.Cards` has
  no card list — it is the vocabulary, not the corpus — so the linter can only refuse an empty name
  (L036). **The pipeline must refuse a `BecomesCopyOfNamed` whose `Name` is not a card in the corpus**
  (ask 4 below): a misspelled name would otherwise reach a file and copy nothing.
- **`Targets` is a predicate on the spell, not a field on `ObjectFilter`.** It holds a whole filter,
  which is the shape `Also` exists for, and only a handful of cards say it; a fixed field would sit
  unread on every filter in the corpus.
- **The cast-from-zone restriction is two optional `ObjectFilter` fields, not a record.** It describes
  the spell, so it belongs with the other things a spell filter says, and `CantCastSpells` already
  takes that filter. As `ZoneChange.From`/`NotFrom` did before it, the allowed zone and the forbidden
  zone are one printed line read two ways, and L038 refuses both at once.
- **`Matches(Self, IsHarnessed)` is the one spelling of "as long as this is harnessed", and no
  condition was added.** A `IsHarnessedCondition` would be a second spelling of a sentence that
  already has one, and every "as long as [this] is [designated]" line — suspected, goaded, prepared,
  the ring-bearer — is written the same way. The test asserts the record does **not** exist, so a
  later wave cannot add it by accident.
- **Lint codes L035–L038**: L035 the secret choice and its readers apart, L036 the empty copied name,
  L037 a `Targets` predicate on something that cannot target, L038 the two cast-from zones together
  and either of them on a permanent. They live in `Linter.Wave23.cs` (93 lines); `Linter.cs` is 497
  lines, unchanged but for the `CheckWave23` call and the `IsConsumer` list.
- **`RuleRef`s added**: `Searches` 701.19, `ChooseNumberSecretly`/`HighestChosen`/`LowestChosen`/
  `PlayerWhoChose` 700.2, `CardsDrawnThisTurn`/`LandsEnteredThisTurn` 121.1, `BecomesCopyOfNamed`
  707.9, `Targets` 115.1.

## Asks (outside `MtgEngine.Cards/**`)

1. **E-series (trigger system)** — the `Searches` event (row 1), which needs the search itself to be
   an event the trigger system sees, not the shuffle or the card found.
2. **E-series (interpreter and the question road)** — the secret simultaneous choice (row 2): one
   question per player asked without any of them seeing another's answer, and the numbers stored per
   player under the key for the two amounts and the player reference to read back.
3. **E-series (turn record)** — the two per-player per-turn counts (row 3), kept as events happen and
   reset each turn; and (layer system) `BecomesCopyOfNamed` in layer 1, which needs a **printed-card
   lookup by name** rather than a board query (row 4).
4. **Pipeline** — the corpus check on `BecomesCopyOfNamed.Name`: refuse a translation whose name is
   not a card in the corpus, the way the strict parser refuses an unknown member. The linter cannot do
   it (no card list in `MtgEngine.Cards`), and without it a misspelled name copies nothing silently.
5. **P-series (reference)** — **nothing.** Every addition is a new kind under a base the generated
   reference already sections (`Trigger`, `Amount`, `PlayerRef`, `Effect`, `ContinuousEffect`,
   `Predicate`), and `ObjectFilter` is already one of `VocabularyReference`'s root records, so its two
   new parameters are listed automatically. No hand edit to `VocabularyReference.cs`.
6. **Coordinator / pipeline** — refresh both `system.md` files (the prompt text changed, so the prompt
   version moved) and redo the seven cards on three models. A reading that spells any of these as an
   `Unsupported`, as an `If` around the record, or as the second spelling the lines now forbid is a
   real disagreement and not a canonical rewrite.

## Not done in this box

- **No `Searches` field for the zone or for whose library.** See the decisions; the corpus prints
  neither.
- **No `Min`/`Max` lint on `ChooseNumberSecretly`.** A card could print either bound alone, and a
  crossed pair (`Min` above `Max`) is a comparison of two `Amount`s, which the linter cannot make
  without evaluating them.
- **No `HighestChosen` for anything but a secret choice.** An open choice is still `ChosenNumber`;
  nothing in the slice reads the highest of a set of open choices.
- **No `IsLegendary`/`ExceptThisAbility` on `BecomesCopyOfNamed`.** Neither is printed beside a named
  copy in the corpus; when one is, it is the same field `BecomesCopyOf` carries, on this record.
- **`Targets` cannot say "targets only".** "Target spell that targets only a permanent you control"
  would need a count over the announced targets, and no card in the slice prints it.
- **No `CastFrom` on the `SpellCast` trigger.** "Whenever an opponent casts a spell from their
  graveyard" would be the same two fields on that trigger's filter, which already takes an
  `ObjectFilter`, so it costs nothing to add when a card asks; nothing in the slice does.
