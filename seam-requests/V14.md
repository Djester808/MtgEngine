## Decisions taken here

- **The attack tax is a `Cost` field on `CantAttack` and carries no per-creature field.** The
  scaling is an `Amount` inside the cost — `PayManaCost(Times(AttackersAgainst(You), 2))` — and both
  halves already existed; a `Per` field would be a second place to say the same thing and would sit
  null on every other card. `Unless` (a board exception, "unless it's a Zombie") and `UnlessPays`
  are two different printed lines, so **both set is refused (L040)**: two exceptions on one
  restriction would let an attack through by satisfying either, which no card says.
- **`MayCastAsThoughFlash` is a permission on a player, not a keyword on a card.** The printed line
  is "you may cast green creature spells as though they had flash": there is no object to grant
  Flash to at the time it matters — the card is in a hand. `What` is optional so "you may cast
  spells as though they had flash" needs no filter.
- **`AttackingWhom` is a `PlayerRef` and not a `Selector`.** CR 508.4 lets the controller choose the
  player, one of their planeswalkers, or a battle they protect; the printed phrase in the corpus is
  always "attacking that player or a planeswalker they control", which is one player's side of the
  board. A selector would make the planeswalker a target-shaped choice it is not. Set without
  `Attacking` it is a contradiction (L040).
- **`AnOpponent` is a `PlayerRef` and not a `PlayerFilter` on `Controls`.** Every condition that
  takes a player takes a `PlayerRef`, and the existential reading is needed by `Controls`,
  `HasCardsInHand` and their kin alike; a field on one of them would have to be added to each.
  **L041 refuses it as a `PlayerRef` property of anything that is not a `Condition`** (checked by
  reflection over each node's `PlayerRef`-typed properties), so it can be read and never acted on.
- **The two life amounts are two records, not one with a flag.** `TriggeringDamage` set the shape,
  and a flag would let a life-gain trigger read a life-loss amount by flipping a bool — the exact
  mistake L039 exists to catch. **L039 is the pairing check, both ways**, and admits a
  `DelayedTrigger` the ability creates, as L028 does for damage.
- **`IsModified` is one predicate.** CR 700.9 gives the word one definition; an `AnyOf` of three
  predicates would be three chances to drop the Aura's controller clause, and `WithCounter` alone
  (the reading two translators wrote) silently misses the Equipment and the Aura.
- **`Step.Main` is appended last in the enum**, so no stored file's step shifts even though the
  serializer writes names. `PrecombatMain`/`PostcombatMain` stay for the cards that name one.
- **`Destroy.RememberAs` needed no new lint**: `Linter`'s producer walk already picks up any
  `RememberAs` property, so an unread key is L003 and a `Remembered` key nothing produces is L003 —
  both are tested here.
- **RuleRefs added**: `Destroy` 701.8 (the brief said 701.7, which is Create), `TriggeringLifeGained`
  / `TriggeringLifeLost` 603.10, `IsModified` 700.9, `CantAttack` 508.1h (the brief said 508.1e,
  which is banding; 508.1g–508.1h are the attack costs), `MayCastAsThoughFlash` 702.8,
  `CreateToken` 701.7 (unchanged), `AnOpponent` 102.2 (102.1 is "a player"), `Step.Main` 505.1.
  Every number was read in `comprehensive-rules.txt` before it was written.

## Not done in this box — for V15

- **No card ids in the table.** The box went to the nine records; each was found by its printed
  phrase in the brief, and I did not spend the remaining calls searching `cards/` for the id of each
  one. **V15 or the coordinator should attach the id to each row before the redo**, by searching
  `cards/` for the printed phrase (never trusting an id handed over).
- **No lint for `Step.Main`.** A `SkipStepEffect(Main)` or an `AtStep(Main)` on a card that named a
  specific main phase cannot be told apart from the general reading by the linter; the one-spelling
  line carries it instead.
- **No `TriggeringLifeGained` for a life-*total* trigger.** "Whenever your life total changes" is not
  in the corpus; when it is, it is a third event and not a flag on these two.
- **`MayCastAsThoughFlash` cannot say "as though it had flash" about one named spell** ("you may cast
  this from your graveyard as though it had flash" is that permission plus `MayPlayFromZone`, two
  sentences and two records) and cannot say "any time you could cast an instant" for an *activated
  ability*, which is a different permission nothing in the slice prints.
- **`CantAttack.UnlessPays` has no "for each" of anything but the attackers.** The two other printed
  taxes ("pays {1} for each enchantment you control", Sphere of Safety's shape) are the same field
  with a different `Amount` inside, so they cost nothing — but neither is in these slices and neither
  is tested.
- **`AnOpponent`'s L041 is a reflection check over `PlayerRef` properties.** It cannot see an
  `AnOpponent` reached through a list of `PlayerRef` (no record has one today); if one is added, the
  check needs the list arm.
