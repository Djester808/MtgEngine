# P20 — the generator's two false failures and the six without a scenario

Wave 19. The generator side is `MtgEngine.Cards.Pipeline/Scenarios/**`, the tests are
`tests/MtgEngine.Pipeline.Tests/ScenarioStageTests.Wave19.cs` (each road played through
`Subsystems.Wave2()`). Three cards moved to verified in the box: `13575cf9` (a source that puts
itself on top of its owner's library), `948217ac` (counter unless its controller pays), and — as a
road, proven by its test but blocked on this card by § 3 — the graveyard-to-library move.

Every ask below is an engine reading; each card is left **without** a scenario rather than with one
that reports the engine's gap as the card failing (the P16 rule). Each has a test that fails the day
the seam lands, so the withheld road is not forgotten.

## 1. A filter's `permanent` read against a card outside the battlefield (E0/E-series)

`Query.cs:160` reads the field as presence on the battlefield:

```csharp
if (f.Permanent is { } permanent && permanent != (obj.Zone.Zone == Cards.Zone.Battlefield))
```

CR 110.1 makes that right for a filter with no `in`. It is wrong for one bound to another zone:
CR 110.4c says a *permanent card* is a card with one or more permanent card types, wherever it is.
`1b9f9f5b` (Sevinne's Reclamation, "return target permanent card with mana value 3 or less from your
graveyard to the battlefield") was staged with a basic land filler in the caster's graveyard and
played: **the card stayed in the graveyard** (`InZone(aim-1, Battlefield): Graveyard`) — no card in a
graveyard can match `permanent: true`, so the spell had nothing legal to move.

Ask: a filter whose `in` is not the battlefield reads `permanent` as CR 110.4c — the object's card
types — rather than as its zone. This is the same reading P19 § 1 asked for on `e2487868`'s `Choose`
among revealed library cards; one change settles both.

Generator half, withheld: `SmokeFillers.Buried` refuses a filter carrying `Permanent`. Delete that
guard and
`A_permanent_card_in_a_graveyard_gets_no_scenario_until_the_query_reads_a_permanent_card` when it
lands. Two other corpus cards carry the same graveyard filter (`7c2a4fe5`, `b2e950fb`) and verify on
scenarios that never exercise it.

## 2. A planeswalker enters with no loyalty counters (E1/E5-series)

CR 306.5b: a planeswalker enters the battlefield with a number of loyalty counters equal to its
printed loyalty. Nothing in `MtgEngine.Rules` ever writes `Counters.Loyalty` — the only reader is
`Sba/StateBasedActions.Permanents.cs:162`, CR 704.5i — so **every planeswalker is buried the moment
it arrives**. `f78af825` (Elspeth, Storm Slayer, printed loyalty 5) failed exactly that way
(`InZone(Elspeth, Battlefield): Graveyard`); the generator's expectation was right about the card and
the play was wrong.

Ask: the zone-change that puts a permanent onto the battlefield reads `Face.Loyalty` and puts that
many loyalty counters on it as it enters (CR 306.5b, an enter-with-counters replacement like any
other, CR 614.1c). Three corpus cards are planeswalkers (`05e6b243`, `5aa3abf1`, `f78af825`) and none
verifies today.

Generator half, withheld: `SmokeScenario.Of` returns null for a planeswalker face. Delete that arm
and `A_planeswalker_gets_no_scenario_until_it_enters_with_its_loyalty` when it lands.

## 3. Phyrexian mana in a printed cost (E3-series)

The casting system refuses it by name: *"The engine refused CastSpell: Phyrexian mana {G/P} is not
supported yet. (107.4)"*. CR 107.4f: a Phyrexian symbol is paid with the mana it names or with 2
life. `97cabeda` (Noxious Revival, `{G/P}`) is the only corpus card with one, and its own road — "put
target card from a graveyard on top of its owner's library" — is **in and tested**
(`A_card_put_from_a_graveyard_on_top_of_a_library_is_expected_in_the_library` plays it through
`Subsystems.Wave2()`), so this card verifies the day the cost can be paid.

Generator half, withheld: `SmokeScenario.Of` returns null for a printed cost containing `/P`. Delete
that arm and `A_phyrexian_cost_gets_no_scenario_until_the_casting_system_can_pay_one`.

## 4. Left without a scenario on purpose

- **`00d8efa6` (Utopia Sprawl)** — an Aura on a Forest whose "whenever enchanted Forest is tapped for
  mana" trigger adds one mana of a colour the Aura chose as it entered. Three things are missing, not
  one: `Enchant` reads only a `Simple` filter, so `subtypes: [Forest]` is refused before anything
  else; **there is no scenario step that taps a land for mana** (`ScenarioStep` has cast, activate,
  play-land, attack, block, pass, answer, advance, add-mana and expect-refused — `AddManaStep` puts
  mana in a pool without tapping anything, so it fires no `TappedForMana`); and the pool afterwards
  is the land's own mana beside the trigger's, in an order the generator would be guessing. Ask, if
  it is wanted: a `TapForManaStep(player, card, abilityIndex)` in the runner, and then the generator
  can enchant a Forest filler, tap it and name the pool. Not asked for in this box — one card.
- **`30a0a6c2` (Torment of Hailfire)** — a punisher: *repeat X times, each opponent loses 3 life
  unless that player sacrifices a nonland permanent or discards a card*. The `Unless` here is paid
  with a `ChooseCost` of two options, and which one the opponent takes is answered by the runner's
  first-legal-option rule — a sacrifice with an empty battlefield is not legal, a discard is, and
  which of them the play shows is the runner's choice and not the definition's. The generator can
  assert neither half ("loses 3 life" is precisely what does *not* always happen), so it gets none.
  The `{1}`-payment road in § 5 below is the shape that *can* be asserted: one mana cost, no pool.
- **`30ec73ad` (change the target of a spell with a single target)** — as P16 § 3 and P19 § 5: no
  filler spell has a target, so nothing on the stack has a target to change and a scenario would
  always pass. Still declined; one card, and a targeted filler is the runner's to invent.
- **`c20a96f7`** and **`e2487868`** stay as P19 left them. `e2487868` is unblocked by § 1 above:
  its withheld arms are in the wave-17 history of `SmokeScenario.Remembered.cs`.
