# P27 — the two unwinnable scenarios, and why Mox Diamond is not a scenario defect

Wave 28. Owns `MtgEngine.Cards.Pipeline/Scenarios/**`, `Verify/**`,
`tests/MtgEngine.Pipeline.Tests/ScenarioStageTests*.cs`, `VerifyCommandTests*.cs`.
Anchor `ScenarioStageTests`; four new tests in `ScenarioStageTests.Wave28.cs`.

## 1. Lotus Field `134d5b82` — done, no ask

E9v § 1's first ask. `SmokeScenario.Enters` gained a `Sacrifices` road beside `Returns` and
`Edicts`: a "when this enters, sacrifice N of a kind" trigger stands N bodies of the kind the
filter admits on the caster's battlefield **as bystanders** — placed by the setup, so they are
older objects than the card under test (CR 400.7) and the first-legal answer takes them
(CR 701.16a; the options are in object order). Each is named in the caster's graveyard and the
graveyard is counted, so the play says the sacrifice happened rather than only that the land is
still standing.

Without the road, the land's own trigger has exactly one land to take — itself — CR 701.16b makes
it sacrifice as many as it can, and `InZone Lotus Field Battlefield` cannot hold. That is what the
card was failing on, and the engine was right both times.

**Tests:** `A_land_whose_arrival_sacrifices_lands_stands_the_lands_it_will_take` (two lands stood,
both named in the graveyard, the land under test on the battlefield) and
`The_bodies_a_sacrifice_stands_are_the_kind_its_own_filter_admits` (a creature filter stands a
creature, so the road is not "stand two of whatever the card is"). Falsified by removing the
`Sacrifices` call: the scenario then carries no graveyard expectation at all.

## 2. Gamble `a54f0869` — done, no ask

E9v § 1's second ask, and the reason it is not "regenerate the file": the generator had two
defects, and only one of them was about the numbers.

- **The random discard was not read at all.** `Discard`'s arm required `N: Fixed`, and Gamble's
  record names no number, so the discard fell through to `Remembers` and asserted nothing — hence
  the impossible hand 4 / graveyard 1. `Discarded(discard)` now reads CR 701.8a's "a card" as one,
  and the two counts (a card out of the hand, a card into the graveyard) are the same whoever
  chose it.
- **The search must not name the card it found.** CR 701.9b: the discard is the game's choice, and
  it may take the card the search just put in the hand — it did, which is the `InZone was Graveyard`
  the card was failing on. `SmokeScenario.AtRandom` reads every effect of the resolution **before**
  any of them is asserted (the discard is written after the search) and sets `SmokeBoard.DiscardsAtRandom`;
  `Found` then withholds the `InZone` for a card searched **into a hand** only. A card the search
  put onto the battlefield or into a graveyard is out of a discard's reach and is still named.
- **The hand is counted even though the deltas cancel.** `SmokeBoard.HandHolds()`, the twin of
  `LifeHolds` and for the same reason: one card in and one card out net to nothing, and the size
  the hand is left at is the thing that holds however the random discard fell.

**Tests:** `A_random_discard_does_not_name_the_card_the_search_found` (no expectation names the
stocked card; hand 3, graveyard 2 and the shuffle are asserted) and, as the falsifier,
`A_chosen_discard_still_names_the_card_the_search_found` — the same card with `By: Owner` keeps the
`InZone` naming it, so the silence is about the randomness and not about searches.

**Still failing on the card, and the reason is not the scenario — unexplained, hand this on.**
`verify cards --only a54f0869-…` now reports `ZoneCount was 0`, not the old
`InZone was Graveyard`, so the naming defect is closed and the counts are what is left. The
expectations the file now carries, in order, are `Shuffled A`, `InZone Gamble Graveyard`,
`ZoneCount A Hand 3`, `ZoneCount A Library 7`, `ZoneCount A Graveyard 2`, `StackEmpty`. The
`InZone` before them passes, which puts Gamble in the graveyard, so the count that read **0**
cannot be the graveyard's — it is the hand's (or, less plausibly, the library's). A hand of nought
is four cards gone where the record moves one: setup hand is Gamble plus three filler lands, the
cast takes Gamble, the search adds one, `EffectInterpreter.Actions`'s `Discard` arm computes
`count = Math.Min(hand.Count, Number(run, discard.N))` and `Number`'s `whenMissing` is **1**, so
the engine and the generator agree the discard is one card. I could not reconcile that with the
observed 0 inside the box and I did not guess at it: the next agent should run this one scenario
through the runner in-process and read the event log, because if the play really empties the hand
that is an `Interpreter/**` defect (CR 701.8a) and not a count to be relaxed. **Do not "fix" it by
dropping the hand expectation** — `HandHolds` is there because the two moves cancel, and a
scenario that says nothing about the hand is exactly the one that passed over this.

## 3. Ask — Mox Diamond `f3c5978a` is **not** the harness's hand (`Interpreter/**` with `State/**`)

The wave-27 decision reads "its remaining failure is the harness hand holding no land to discard".
**Measured: the hand already holds three.** `SmokeBoard.Build` stocks the caster's hand with
`HandSize` (3) `ScenarioFiller.Land` cards beside the card under test, every one of them a Land by
its type line, and `ObjectCosts.Choice` admits them (`CardZone.Hand`, by ownership, the filter
`{types:[Land]}`). Nothing in `Scenarios/**` can make that hand more payable, and **no number of
lands closes it** — which is the point:

E8e ask 2 is the whole of it (CR 614.5). The paid branch's `Then` is `Move(Self → Battlefield)`,
which goes back through `ZoneSystem.Replacing` with a **fresh** `used` set, so the same
`ReplacementAbility` matches its own modified event and asks again. `ScenarioAnswers.FirstLegal`
says yes again, a second land is discarded, and so on: the loop eats one land per turn of the
crank until the hand has none, the payment finally fails, and `Else` puts the artifact in the
graveyard. `InZone was Graveyard` is the **last** iteration talking, not the first.

So the ask is the one DECISIONS already scheduled for wave 28 and no other: the used keys
travelling on the `EffectRun`/`EffectContext` into `ReplacementSystem.Replace` and restored on
resumption, the way `RememberedPrefix` restores CR 608.2h's memory. Folder `Interpreter/**` with
`State/**`; `Replacement/**` cannot hold the fact, as E8e showed. **Until then Mox Diamond's
scenario is correct and failing**, which is what a scenario is for — it is not withdrawn and
nothing was padded to make it pass.

Verified on the card, at the wave-27 engine (`9781a597f8c8ddfa`):
`verify cards --only f3c5978a-…` → `FAILED 1/2: InZone was Graveyard`. Scenario 2 (the activated
ability) passes.

## 4. Item 3 — the cards without a scenario: **not reached**, and what the next agent should know

The box closed on items 1 and 2. What was measured rather than guessed: **84 of the 512 card files
carry no `scenarios` array on disk** (a `node` pass over `cards/*.json`), against the 52 the
wave-27 verify run reported — the two numbers differ because `VerifyCommand.Regenerated` builds the
scenarios at verify time and only writes them back for the cards it ran, so the on-disk absence is
a superset. Of the 84, 34 are `Disputed` (no agreed reading to generate from — those belong to the
translation rounds, not to a generator road) and 50 are `Translated`.

No card in that list was staged and none is recorded here with a reason, because a reason read off
a name rather than off the definition is exactly the "sentence dump" mistake. The next P-series
agent should run `verify cards` once for the authoritative list, then group it by the record the
generator refuses on (`Assert`'s `Destroy or Sacrifice or Exile or PutCounters or Counter or Tap`
arm and `Remembers`' default are where most of them die) rather than card by card.
