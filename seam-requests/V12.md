## Decisions taken here

- **The optional additional cost is a `ContinuousEffect`, not a new ability kind or a rider on
  `CostModifier`.** It is a static ability's effect on other objects (CR 604.1), like every other
  cost-changing effect in this vocabulary, and it has to be a record of its own because the thing it
  changes is *which costs exist* rather than *what a cost is* — a `CostModifier` with an "only if you
  paid" flag would have no way to say what the payment was.
- **What paying buys is `IfPaid`, a `ContinuousEffect`, and it is required.** Typing it as
  `CostModifier` would close the door on the next card that buys something else; leaving it optional
  would admit a cost no caster would ever pay, so L030 refuses a null. It is a `ContinuousEffect`, so
  it carries a `kind` like every other one and the output rules need no exception.
- **`OnlyColor` is a field on `CostModifier`, not a second record.** "Costs {G} less" and "costs {1}
  less" are the same sentence with a different symbol; a `ColoredCostModifier` beside it would be a
  second spelling the translator has to choose between. `GenericOnly` and `OnlyColor` describe the
  same half of a cost and L030 refuses both.
- **The excess is a rider on the damage, not a second `DealDamage` and not an `ExcessDamage`
  amount.** An amount would have to be read by an instruction that does not know what was lethal to
  what; a second `DealDamage` deals a second, independent lot of damage. `ExcessIf` exists because the
  card gates the redirection and nothing else: spelling the gate as an `If` around two copies of the
  damage instruction writes one printed sentence twice, and that was one of the readings the
  translators disagreed over.
- **The mode price is on `Mode` and the budget on `ChooseMode`.** A price is a property of the printed
  mode; a budget is a property of the choice. Neither means anything alone, so L032 requires them
  together — every mode priced when there is a budget, and a budget whenever any mode is priced.
- **The draw exception is a flag, not a condition.** A `TriggeredAbility.If` cannot see which draw of
  the step this was: by the time the condition is read the draw has happened and the step's count is
  the game's, not the card's. It has to sit in the trigger, beside `FirstEachTurn`, whose opposite it
  is (L033).
- **The mandatory "if you do" is `PayCost.Then`, not `MayPay.Mandatory`.** A flag on `MayPay` would
  make the record's own name a lie in half its uses and would leave `MayPay.Else` ("if you don't")
  attached to a cost the player never chose. `PayCost` already is the mandatory cost; it gained the
  half it was missing. L034 keeps the two from both spelling one sentence.
- **Lint codes L030–L034**, one per gap: L030 the optional additional cost (nothing gated on it; one
  gating another) and the reduction that claims both halves of a cost, L031 `ExcessTo` with nothing
  damaged and `ExcessIf` with nowhere to send the excess, L032 the budget and the prices apart,
  L033 the spared draw claimed as the only draw that counts (and the draw step already excluded),
  L034 the `May` around a `PayCost.Then` and a `Then` that does nothing. They live in
  `Linter.Wave22.cs`; `Linter.cs` calls `CheckWave22` beside `CheckWave19` and is at 495 lines.
- **`RuleRef`s added**: `OptionalAdditionalCost` 118.8, `Mode` 700.2, `Draws` 121.1. `DealDamage`
  keeps 120, `CostModifier` 601.2f, `ChooseMode` 700.2, `PayCost` 118.12.

## Not done in this box

- **No lint that a `CostModifier` with a negative `Delta` is a reduction.** L004 already warns on any
  negative literal, and every reduction in `cards/` carries that warning today; changing the sign
  convention is a corpus-wide rewrite, not a lint added mid-wave.
- **No check that `Mode.Cost` is mana.** A future card could price modes in something else, and the
  budget reads whatever the cost's symbols are.
- **No `ExcessTo` on `DealDividedDamage`.** No card in the slice divides damage and redirects its
  excess; when one does, it is the same two fields on that record, not a new one.
- **`PayCost.Then` has no `Else`.** "If you don't" beside a mandatory cost is not a sentence any card
  in the slice prints — the player has no choice to make — and `Unless` already carries the
  unpaid-cost half where a card does print it.
