## Decisions taken here

- **Item 2 is `PayLifeEqualToManaValueInstead` on `MayPlayFromZone`**, as the brief proposed, and not
  a new `ContinuousEffect`. The life payment is meaningless without the permission ("if you cast a
  spell this way"), so it is a rider on the permission, beside the waiver it is the alternative of.
  `SpellsCostLifeInsteadOfMana(Who, Colors)` stays for the other card shape — every spell of a colour
  from any zone — and is not the spelling of this one. L021: both riders set is a contradiction
  (CR 118.9: a cast pays one alternative cost).
- **Item 3 added `Choose.Among`** rather than a one-spelling line, because the existing spelling has a
  trap the two candidates fell into from both sides: an `IsRemembered` predicate inside a `Choose`'s
  filter reads the filter's `In`, which defaults to the battlefield, so the sonnet reading (no `In`)
  would have chosen among nothing, and the opus reading had to write `In: Library` by hand and would
  break the moment the remembered cards were in exile. With `Among` the candidates carry their own
  zone. `IsRemembered` inside a `Choose`'s filter is now the wrong spelling and the prompt says so;
  it is not linted because the linter does not guess which of two readings a card meant.
- **Item 4: `IsOneOf` was added, and the pilot needed it.** Three pilot cards spell "whenever
  equipped creature dies/attacks/deals combat damage" as `AttachedTo(Self)` inside the trigger's
  filter — `65986c1b-8e51-4604-b685-d82fa7d1263a` and `d79cbc61-6c15-48ea-bbba-3cffb819ccba` are
  **Verified**, `d0901053-6de0-46d0-9ee3-8d40510236c1` is Disputed with both readings agreeing on it.
  The `AttachedTo` doc (V8) says the direction is candidate-first, so those readings describe the
  Auras on the Equipment, and they verify only because `Query.Predicates.cs:37` implements the
  **inverted** direction. That is the wave-13 engine note, now confirmed from the vocabulary side:
  the vocabulary had no correct spelling for "equipped creature" inside a filter, and `IsRemembered`
  cannot say it because nothing remembers the equipped creature. `IsOneOf(Attached)` is that
  spelling; `IsOneOf(Remembered(key))` is deliberately a second spelling of `IsRemembered(key)` and
  the prompt says to keep `IsRemembered` for a key.
- **The fixture at `Fixtures.cs:21` is fixed** to `IsOneOf(Attached)`; no test relied on the inverted
  reading (`LinterTests` and `SerializationTests` use the Clamp fixture for its shape only). A test
  now asserts the fixture carries no `AttachedTo`.
- **`Linter` became `partial`**: `Linter.cs` was at 490 lines, so the two new rules live in
  `Linter.Permissions.cs` and `CheckAbility` calls `CheckPermissions`. New codes: L021 (both
  alternative-cost flags on `MayPlayFromZone`), L022 (`Choose.Among` beside `Filter.In`).
- **`RuleRef`s added**: `MayPlay` 702.75a, `MayPlayFromZone` 118.9, `Choose` 700.2, `IsOneOf` 301.5c.
