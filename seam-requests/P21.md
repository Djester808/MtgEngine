# P21 — the generator reads `Search.Instead`, and a spell that shuffles itself away

Wave 21. Generator side is `MtgEngine.Cards.Pipeline/Scenarios/**`, tests are
`tests/MtgEngine.Pipeline.Tests/ScenarioStageTests.Searches.cs`, `.Remembered.cs`, `.Wave19.cs` and
the new `.Wave21.cs` (each road played through `Subsystems.Wave2()`). Both failures the wave-20
verify left for this box are gone, and two of the seven scenario-less cards are staged.

**No engine ask.** Every reading this box needed was already in: E9o for `Instead`, E9p for the
shuffled self and for a permanent card off the battlefield.

## 1. `Search.Instead` — the destination the card found chooses (CR 701.23a)

`3c1ef404` (Archdruid's Charm) failed `InZone was Battlefield`: the road put the found card in the
search's `To` (hand) while the engine read `Instead` and put the found *land* onto the battlefield
tapped. `SmokeScenario.Searches.cs` now has a `Destination` that reads it the way the interpreter
does — the card the library was stocked with is read against each entry's `Matching` in the order
they are written, and the first that admits it wins; a search with no entries keeps its own `To`.

`SmokeFillers.Admits` is the reading, deliberately three-valued: **null is not "no"**. An entry
whose filter names something the board cannot read against a card — a zone, a controller (CR 108.4:
a card in a library has none), a bound with no number — makes the whole search scenario-less rather
than one that guesses a zone and reports the guess as the card failing. That is the P16 rule applied
to a destination instead of to an effect.

A card that arrives on the battlefield is now also asserted **tapped or untapped** (`TappedIs`),
because "put it onto the battlefield tapped" is half of what those searches promise and nothing was
asserting it. All 38 corpus cards whose search reaches a battlefield or carries an `Instead` were
re-verified one at a time under the new expectation: **38 of 38 verified**.

## 2. `Shuffle { IntoLibrary: Self }` — the generator's expectation was the stale one (CR 608.2m)

`0d96b60b` (Green Sun's Zenith) failed `InZone was Library`. The engine is right and P19 § 2
predicted the fix exactly: since E9p the shuffle is a real move, so by the time the resolution ends
the spell is no longer on the stack and CR 608.2m's graveyard never gets it (CR 400.7). One arm
beside the `Exile { What: Self }` one sets `board.SpellEndsIn = Zone.Library`; `SmokeBoard` already
carried a library and counted it. Guarded on `!PlaysFromBattlefield` so an activation of a source
that shuffles itself away stays `SmokeActivation`'s road and is untouched — there is a test for that.

`ScenarioStageTestsRemembered.A_spell_that_only_shuffles_itself_away_gets_no_scenario_until_the_engine_moves_it`
was the withheld test; it is now `..._is_expected_in_its_owners_library` and plays.
