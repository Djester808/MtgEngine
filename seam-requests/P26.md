## 1. "agreed among all-clean: 35/34" — done, no ask

`Pilot/PilotReport.cs` counted every agreed card over the cards **every** model linted clean, so a
card that agreed on two clean readings while a third linted dirty was in the numerator and not the
denominator — the ratio could pass 100%. The numerator now asks for both, which is the honest
reading of the line's own name (P25 § 3 preferred this over renaming it, because the percentage is
the number the wave reports quote):

```csharp
var allClean = results.Count(AllClean);
var agreedAmongClean = results.Count(r => r.Agreement.Agreed && AllClean(r));
```

`AllClean` is one private predicate, so the two lines cannot drift apart.

**Test:** `PilotTests.Wave27.The_agreed_among_all_clean_line_counts_only_the_cards_every_model_linted_clean`
— two agreed cards, one of which has a dirty third reading. The line reads `1/1 (100.0%)`; before
the change it read `2/1 (200.0%)`, which the test also asserts against by name.

## 2. `VerifyCommand.NotPlayable` — refused per ability, done, no ask

P25 § 4's ask. `NotPlayable` walked the whole definition and refused the card on the **first**
`Unsupported` marker anywhere, so Emergence Zone (`7536eb66`) was counted unsupported for its flash
permission and its `{T}: Add {C}` never played — for seven waves. An ability is its own object
(CR 602.2), and one clause nobody could write says nothing about the ability beside it.

Three parts, all in my files:

- `SmokeScenario.Markers(object node)` — every marker inside one node, **counted by identity**,
  because a reflective walk reaches a face's abilities twice (`Face.Abilities` and the computed
  `Face.AbilityList`); this is P24 § 4's lesson applied to a second reader.
- `SmokeActivation.One` refuses the activation whose own ability carries a marker, and only that
  one — its neighbours keep their scenarios and their indices (the index is the `ActivateStep`'s,
  so the marked ability is skipped, never removed).
- `SmokeScenario.CastIsExpressible(face)` withholds the **cast** scenario when a marker sits
  anywhere outside the face's activated abilities — a spell ability, a trigger, a static ability or
  the printed data are all part of what casting the card does, and a resolution half of which is not
  written down must not get a scenario that cannot fail.
- `VerifyCommand.NotPlayable` now reports the file unsupported only when nothing is left to play:
  every ability marked, or a marker that no ability accounts for (the sum over the abilities is
  compared with the definition's total, so a marker on the printed data is still the card's).

**Tests:** `VerifyCommandTests.Wave27` (a two-ability card runs the clean ability's scenario and not
the marked one's, and reports 0 unsupported; a card whose every ability is marked still reports 1
unsupported with the translator's reason) and
`ScenarioStageTests.Wave27.A_marker_on_one_activated_ability_leaves_its_neighbour_its_scenario`
plus `A_marker_outside_the_activated_abilities_withholds_the_cast_and_not_the_activation`.

**Not verified:** `verify cards --only 7536eb66-959d-4dca-9b75-895572ef733c` was **not run** — the
time box closed before it. The reasoning above says Emergence Zone should now get its land scenario
and its `{T}: Add {C}` activation; that is **unverified against the corpus**, and the next P-series
agent should run that one command first. R2 also warned that a filler basic land has no mana ability
to tap, but Emergence Zone's is a printed ability on the card itself, not an intrinsic one.

## 3. `204b71fc` Elixir of Immortality — road landed, card unverified

The activation scenario asserted `TappedIs` on a source its own ability had shuffled into the
library (CR 400.7 with CR 110.6): `passed 1, total 2` in the file at the wave-26 tip, the activation
being the failure. `SmokeActivation.MovesItself` looked for a bare `Shuffle { IntoLibrary: Self }`,
and Elixir names itself as one member of a **union** — itself and its owner's graveyard together —
so the reading missed it and the tap was asserted anyway.

`MovesItself` is now `Departs(effect)` and returns the **zone**, not a bool: a move to a hand, a
library, a graveyard or exile; a shuffle into a library; an exile of itself — each recognised
through `Itself(selector)`, which admits `Self` directly or as a member of a `Union` and nothing
else (a filter that merely happens to describe the source is not the card naming itself). The
departure is recorded through the board's existing `SmokeBoard.MovesItselfTo`, so the counts follow
it the way the wave-19 `Move { What: Self }` road already made them, and the two readings cannot
assert the departure twice (`board.MovedItselfTo is null` guards both arms).

**Tests:** `ScenarioStageTests.Wave27.An_activation_that_shuffles_its_source_away_asserts_the_zone_and_not_the_tap`
(the exact union shape, asserting `InZone(Library)`, no `TappedIs`, and the 25 life that says the
ability actually resolved) and `An_activation_that_leaves_its_source_standing_still_asserts_the_tap`
(the boundary — without it the change would be a licence to drop the tap from every activation).

**One wave-21 test was rewritten, on purpose.**
`ScenarioStageTestsWave21.An_activation_that_shuffles_its_source_away_is_not_read_as_a_spell_ending_in_a_library`
asserted the **absence** of `InZone(Library)`. That was a proxy for "the spell road did not leak
into an activation", and it only stood while an activation said nothing about its source's zone at
all. It now asserts the same thing the right way round: the library **is** expected, because the
ability put it there (CR 400.7), the graveyard a resolved spell would reach is not (CR 608.2m), and
no tap is asserted (CR 110.6). Checked by mutation: with `Departs` returning null for a union the
test fails on the missing `InZone`; with the guard removed it fails on a doubled one.

**Not verified:** `verify cards --only 204b71fc-4d5f-40dd-903d-56c733171d84` was **not run**. The
anchor's tests are green and the shape is the one the card file's failing expectation named, but a
green anchor is not a verified card (P25 § 5). **No card file under `cards/` was rewritten by me.**
