# P19 — the generator's remaining roads and canonical rules for the twelve

Wave 17. Three of the seven scenario-less pilot cards verify now through real games (Reanimate
`a044474a`, Teferi's Protection `0d4ecdb1`, Malakir Rebirth `a731e87b`); the other four wait on the
seams below or stay without a scenario on purpose. Generator side: `MtgEngine.Cards.Pipeline/Scenarios/**`,
tests in `tests/MtgEngine.Pipeline.Tests/ScenarioStageTests.Remembered.cs` (played through
`Subsystems.Wave2()`); canonical side: `Canonical.cs`, `Canonical.Retranslate.cs`, tests in
`CanonicalTests.Wave17.cs`.

## 1. A permanent card chosen from among revealed library cards is not put onto the battlefield (E-series)

`e2487868` is `Reveal { Top X, rememberAs k1 }`, then `Move { Choose { permanent: true, manaValue <= X,
among: Remembered k1 }, To: Battlefield }`, then `Move { Each { in: Library, IsRemembered k1 and not
IsRemembered k2 }, To: Graveyard }`. The generator staged it (X announced as 2, a basic land filler on
top the choice must take and a filler instant under it that it cannot) and played it through
`Subsystems.Wave2()`: **the chosen card ended in the graveyard with the rest, the battlefield stayed
empty** (`InZone(find-1, Battlefield): Graveyard`, battlefield 0, graveyard 3). The most likely reading:
the query answers `permanent: true` as "is on the battlefield" (CR 110.1) rather than "is a permanent
card" (CR 110.4c: a card with a permanent type), so a card in a library never matches the `Choose`'s
filter, the question has no option, and everything revealed is "not put onto the battlefield". Ask: a
filter's `permanent` read against a card outside the battlefield answers whether it has a permanent card
type. The generator's half is ready and withheld: `SmokeBoard.Reveals`/`Revealed` and
`SmokeFillers.Unfindable` are in; the three arms sit in the wave-17 history of
`SmokeScenario.Remembered.cs` and its test (the reveal-then-choose test asserts "no scenario" until
then, and says so). Withheld rather than shipped, so the card reads "no scenario" and not "failed"
(the P16 rule).

## 2. A spell that shuffles itself into its owner's library ends in the graveyard (E-series)

`Shuffle { IntoLibrary: Self }` as the last step of a resolving spell (`0d96b60b`'s second sentence)
was played: the spell was in the graveyard afterwards (`InZone(<card>, Library): Graveyard`). CR 608.2m
puts a resolved spell in the graveyard only "as the final part of its resolution" if nothing moved it;
a shuffle of `Self` during resolution has moved it (CR 400.7). Ask: the interpreter's `Shuffle` with
`IntoLibrary` reads `Self` as the resolving spell and moves it to the library. When it lands, the
generator's road is one line: `case Shuffle { IntoLibrary: Self, Who: null or You }: board.SpellEndsIn =
Zone.Library;` beside the `Exile { What: Self }` arm in `SmokeScenario.cs`, and the "no scenario" test in
`ScenarioStageTestsRemembered` becomes a `Passes` test like the exile one. `SmokeBoard.SpellEndsIn`
already carries a library and counts it.

## 3. A green creature filler (E-series, `ScenarioFiller`)

`0d96b60b` searches for `{ types: [Creature], colors: [Green], manaValue <= X }`. No filler has a
colour, and inventing one on the pipeline side is inventing a card (`ScenarioFiller.For` is the
runner's). Ask: one coloured creature filler, e.g. a green 1/1 for `{G}`, in `For` **and** `Names`;
`SmokeFillers.Plain` then admits `Colors` the way it admits `Subtypes` (one line, mine). With 2 and 3
in, the card stocks the filler on top, announces X=1, and expects it on the battlefield, `Shuffled(A)`
and the spell in the library.

## Canonical rules for the twelve (report.md "Disagreements")

Three families were one meaning written two ways and are rules now, each with the difference it must
not hide:

| Rule | Settles | Reading |
|---|---|---|
| `spell: true` on `SpellsCastThisTurn.matching` stripped (the `SpellCast` rule, extended) | Veil of Summer | CR 608.1: the amount counts spells already |
| `permanent: true` stripped on a filter with no `in` and no `spell` read in battlefield context | Dawn's Truce | CR 110.1: what is on the battlefield is a permanent; kept in a library, a graveyard or any zone-bound effect (Genesis Wave's `Choose` keeps it) |
| an effect's optional `by` of You stripped like `who` (`Reorder`, `Goad`) — never a trigger's `By` | Sensei's Divining Top (the sonnet vs opus half) | CR 109.5 |

Real disagreements, left as written: **Caged Sun** (`chosenColor` true vs false), **Fog** (`Each` vs
`Self`), **Archdruid's Charm** and **Akroma's Will** (V10 vocabulary), **Rishkar's Expertise** (two
abilities vs one), **Chrome Mox** (`May` around the exile vs a mandatory exile — the choice is not
inside `Exile`), **Utopia Sprawl** (`ofChosenColorKey` on the trigger narrows what it fires for).

Two are one meaning but need the face to fold and were not done in the box: **Myriad Landscape**
(`WouldEnterBattlefield { also: IsThisObject }` vs `{ types: [Land] }` with `selfOnly`, on a land — both
are "this land enters", CR 614.1c; a face-aware rule would drop a self-only enter's subject when it is
`IsThisObject` or the face's own types) and **Sword of Feast and Famine** (`types: [Creature]` beside
`IsOneOf(Attached)` on an Equipment's trigger — CR 301.5c makes the equipped permanent a creature;
the third reading, with no attached predicate at all, is a real difference). **Sensei's Divining Top**
(the fable half) re-reads `Top(3)` where the others reorder the remembered `LookAt` — the brief's
"keyed target versus the re-read"; a Sequence-level rule (a `Remembered k` used once, in the step right
after the `LookAt` that remembers `k`, is that `LookAt`'s selector) was not written in the box.
