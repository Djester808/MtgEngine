# Excluded cards

"Every card" means every card legal in at least one sanctioned format, minus the cards on
this list. A card is added here by the coordinator only, with its reason, and the dashboard
counts excluded cards separately from unimplemented ones. A card whose only mechanic is
itself does **not** belong here; it gets its own primitive.

The categories follow the cards XMage declines for the same reasons. Names are added as
each is confirmed against the corpus; the category is the policy.

| Category | Reason | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Subgames | a game inside the game needs a second engine instance and a second table | Shahrazad |
| Physical dexterity | the effect depends on a physical action at the table | Chaos Orb, Falling Star |
| Watching players | the card reads what players say, wear or do outside the game state | silver-bordered "say a word" cards |
| Ante | the format the mechanic belongs to is not sanctioned | Contract from Below, Bronze Tablet |
| Art, artist and expansion symbol | the card reads print characteristics the engine does not carry | silver-bordered art-matters cards |
| Draft matters | the effect depends on a draft that did not happen | Conspiracy draft-matters cards |
| Outside participants | the card involves someone who is not a player | Kindslaver-style cards |
